How to use

  • Click a dot to add or remove a note.
  • Right-click a dot to cycle its type (open hi-hat, accent, ghost, cross-stick, flam, buzz, rimshot, ride bell, hi-hat foot…).
  • Drag the slider at the right of a part to set that part's volume.
  • Click More drums to show or hide the Tom 1/2/3, Ride, Crash and Cowbell parts.
  • Pick a subdivision on the left to change the grid.
  • Use +/− next to BARS to add or remove bars.
  • Press the triangle to play; adjust BPM and swing. Spacebar toggles playback.
  • Metronome: the ON/OFF button toggles it, 4th/8th/16th sets the pulse, Sound picks its tone, and Vol sets its volume.
  • The header's Dark / Light button switches between bright and dark mode (your choice is remembered).
  • Stickings, Permutations, Grooves, Download SVG/PNG, Export MIDI, Import MIDI, Print, Share.
